01 / THE VISION

Two Days. One Intelligence.

Intellenza 2K26 brings competition, creativity and hands-on learning into one AI-powered experience.

INTELLENZA 2K26 is a technical symposium designed for students who want to build, compete, communicate and think beyond the ordinary. Inspired by Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, the experience combines technology with creativity.


14 October 2026 is the main symposium day. The following day, 15 October 2026, continues the experience with a dedicated workshop focused on practical learning.
